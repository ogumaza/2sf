2sf 0.2.0 rips games that play their music with the sound archive player of Nintendo's SDK, from a sound archive (SDAT). Most DS games use that player. The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass and The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks are the first games checked.

`2sfrip` runs on Windows 10 or later and requires no runtime DLLs. For macOS, the README explains how to build from source.

## New

- **Games on the SDK's sound archive player.** For a game other than Beat City, `2sfrip` looks for a sound archive and for the SDK's sound routines in the game's code. It then runs every mini on its model of the DS. The command that prepares the sequence has to name the sequence's data and its bank in memory, and the mini has to play for 5 seconds without the game changing the sequence or reading data that the set leaves out. If a mini fails, `2sfrip` refuses the game.
- **The library.** The library contains the files that the game reads before its sound starts, and the sound archive cut down to the data of the set's sequences. The ARM9 program and the overlays that the library keeps are stored decompressed.
- **The stub.** The first instruction of the sound library's main routine, which the game calls once a frame, becomes a branch to the stub. On the first call, the stub loads the sequence that the mini names with the game's loader and starts it. Then it waits for each frame and runs the rest of the main routine, as the game does. A mini is the sequence's number.
- **Names and lengths.** Songs are named as in the sound archive, or `SEQ_` and the sequence's number where the archive has no names. `--only` matches those names. The length counts from the boot, so it includes the time the game takes to set up its sound. A sequence that loops plays two passes of its loop and fades over 10 seconds. One that ends gets a fade of 1 second. A sequence that plays no notes gets no mini.
- **Music and sound effects.** In an archive with names, a sequence whose name and player don't mark it as music counts as a sound effect, and `--effects` adds it. Where no sequence counts as music, every sequence outside the sequence archives does.

## Supported games

|---|---|---|

| The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass | USA (NTR-AZEE) | The SDK's sound archive player |
| The Legend of Zelda: Spirit Tracks | USA (NTR-BKIE) and Europe (NTR-BKIP), revisions 0 and 1 | The SDK's sound archive player |

Other games on the SDK's player may rip as well. The table lists the games that have been checked.

## Known limitations

- `2sfrip` doesn't rip streams, the recorded audio that many games keep in the sound archive beside the sequences.
- Sequences in sequence archives, where most sound effects are, aren't ripped.
- Games on other sound drivers, such as Procyon Studio's, will follow.
- Beat City's songs other than the title screen's are named by number.
- 2sf has no player in this release. A set requires a 2SF player that answers the cartridge's read commands from the image.
